Estimated Yearly Amount

Commission and recurring income (RRSP match, vacation accrual) are annualized as if this month repeats all year. Statutory holiday and personal day pay are projected using the counts set in About you, above, instead — so checking those boxes this month doesn't skew the yearly total.

Monthly Estimated Net Pay

Total commission after RRSP, QPP, QPIP, EI, and estimated income tax. Vacation accrual, CEUs, and employer benefits aren't cash yet, so they're not in this number.

    This is an estimate for your own planning, not an official pay statement. Your actual pay is set by payroll's calculation — if this number looks off, bring it to payroll rather than relying on this figure.
